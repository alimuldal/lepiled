## 2. Step-by-Step Build Sequence

1. **Reflow `led_mcpcb` Strips (4×):** Apply leaded/lead-free paste using the segmented thermal-pad stencil apertures. **CRITICAL POLARITY CHECK:** `D_UV` (`SST-10-UV`) has `BTM NOTCH = CATHODE (K)`; `D_VIS` (Cree `XP-E2`) has `BTM NOTCH = ANODE (A)` — opposite bottom-pad notches! Verify every LED with a multimeter diode test (`~3.3–3.6 V` UV, `~2.6–3.0 V` Vis) after reflow.
2. **Prepare Core & External Heatsinks:** Cut `MECH-HS-CORE` (`40 × 40 × 3 mm` Al 6063) to `165.0 mm`, drill bottom cross-pin bores and top `±1.5 mm` slots, mask the 4 LED mounting stripes and top end face, and spray matte-black high-emissivity coating (`MECH-COAT-1`). Drill/tap both `SK 578/50 SA` hubs (`4× M3` at `R = 8.5 mm`; drill `Ø6.5 mm` center through-bore on top heatsink only).
3. **Mount MCPCBs & Wire Series Strings:** Apply thin `Arctic MX-4` (`MECH-TIM-1`) under each strip and bolt all 4 strips to `MECH-HS-CORE` with `8× M3 × 8 mm` DIN 912 SHCS (`MECH-FAST-1`). Daisy-chain the 4 UV pads (`UV+ -> D1 -> D2 -> D3 -> D4 -> UV-`) and 4 Vis pads (`VIS+ -> D5 -> D6 -> D7 -> D8 -> VIS-`) using `22 AWG` silicone wire, crimp `JST SXH-001T-P0.6` contacts into `2× JST XHP-2` plugs (`HARN-01`), and verify `Pin 1 = +` (`UV+`/`VIS+`), `Pin 2 = -` (`UV-`/`VIS-`) against the `B.SilkS` labels.
4. **Driver PCB & Thermal Interface:** Line the three `2.5 mm` relief pockets on `MECH-ADAPT-BOT` (`J1/TP1/TP2`, `J2/J3`, `J4`) with Kapton tape (`MECH-MISC-1`). Cut matching relief holes in the `34 × 44 × 0.5 mm` `TG-A6200` silicone gap pad (`MECH-TIM-PCB`). Mount `driver_pcb` to `MECH-ADAPT-BOT` using `4× M2.5 × 6 mm` SHCS with **flat M2.5 nylon washers (`Ø5.0 mm OD`, `MECH-FAST-2`)** under the screw heads. Verify `> 10 MΩ` continuity isolation between `VIN_20V` / `VBUS` and `MECH-ADAPT-BOT`.
5. **Switch & Power Entry:** Install `SW1` (`100SP3T1B1M1QEH`) + `SW1_BOOT` (`759D02000`) through the `+X` wall of `MECH-CAP-BOT`, wire to `JST XHP-3` (`HARN-02`: `1=UV`, `2=COM/GND`, `3=ALL`), and plug into `J4`. For Option A (`mech_cap_bot`), fit `MECH-GLAND-1` (`M16`) and solder a captive `18–24 V` DC / PD-trigger pigtail to `TP1 (+)` and `TP2 (GND)`. For Option B (`mech_cap_bot_usbc`), align `J1` with the `-Y` direct-mate tunnel.

## 3. Staged Bench Bring-Up (Laptop USB-C PD Charger)

1. **Unplugged Shorts Check:** Measure resistance between `VIN_20V` and `GND` (`> 5 kΩ`), `VBUS` and `GND` (`> 10 kΩ`), and across `J2`/`J3` (open).
2. **USB-C PD 20 V Negotiation (No LEDs Connected, `SW1` = OFF):** Plug in a standard `45–100 W` USB-C PD laptop charger into `J1`. Confirm green LED `D15` illuminates steadily, `V(VBUS) = 20.0 V ± 1 V`, `V(VIN_20V) = 20.0 V ± 1 V`, and `V(V_REF) = 2.500 V ± 0.01 V`.
3. **5 V UVLO Rejection Check:** Plug in a legacy `5 V`-only USB charger. Confirm `V(U1_CTRL) < 50 mV`, `V(U2_CTRL) < 50 mV`, and both channels remain completely disabled.
4. **LED Load Test (UV400 Glasses On!):** Reconnect the `20 V` PD charger, plug in `J2` (UV) and `J3` (Vis), and test all three `SW1` positions:
   - `OFF` (center): `I_in < 15 mA`, all 8 LEDs dark.
   - `4-UV`: `D1..D4` lit cleanly, `I_in ≈ 0.25 A @ 20 V` (`~5.0 W`), `V(R3) = 100 mV ± 6 mV` (`I_UV ≈ 333 mA`).
   - `8-ALL`: All 8 LEDs lit, `I_in ≈ 0.45 A @ 20 V` (`~9.1 W`), `V(R3) ≈ 100 mV`, `V(R4) ≈ 100 mV`.
5. **Final Enclosure Seal:** Insert `MECH-GASKET-1..3` silicone O-rings, tighten the `2× M4 × 16 mm` CSK bulkhead bolts (with nylon CSK insulating washers), seat `MECH-TUBE-1` glass tube and `MECH-CAP-TOP`, fit `4× 0.5 mm` SS shims (`MECH-FAST-3W`), torque `MECH-ADAPT-TOP` + `MECH-HS-EXT-TOP` + `MECH-EYE-1`, and secure bottom `MECH-ADAPT-BOT` + `MECH-HS-EXT-BOT` via `4× M3 × 8 mm` SHCS.
