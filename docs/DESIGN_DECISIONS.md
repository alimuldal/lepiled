# LepiLED DIY Maxi Switch — Engineering Design Decisions & Review Log

This document tracks the architectural evolution of the open-source DIY fanless "Maxi Switch" LepiLED moth lamp, including every major finding from all five independent adversarial hardware reviews, the user's design inputs, and the resulting engineering decisions locked into **Rev 4.8**.

## 2. Adversarial Review Findings (Rev 4.0) — Carbon & Opus 5.5

Following Rev 4.0, two independent fresh-eyes adversarial reviews were conducted across all electrical, PCB, CAD, thermal, and BOM artifacts using two distinct frontier models (**Carbon** and **Opus 5.5**). Every actionable finding is tracked below alongside its Rev 4.1 resolution.

## 3. User Design Inputs & Locked Architectural Decisions

Four key architectural trade-offs from the reviews plus three simplification proposals were presented to the user. Every decision below reflects direct user input:

| Decision ID | Topic | User Input | Locked Rev 4.1 Engineering Choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1A + S2-A** | Power input & reverse-blocking (`TP1/TP2` Aux vs USB-C Only) | *"I don't care about wiring power tool batteries, let's stick to USBC only."* | **USB-C PD (`J1`) ONLY.** Removed `TP1/TP2` Aux pads, `F2`, `D13`, `Q1B`, and `M16_GLAND` cap variant. Single `40 V` `DMN4035L-7` (`Q1`) gives zero OR-ing diode loss, zero back-feed path, and works seamlessly with both a garden laptop USB-C charger and a portable USB-C PD power bank. |
| **2A** | Driver PCB chassis grounding (`H1–H4`) | Selected **2A** (isolated unplated NPTH mounting holes). | Unplated `Ø2.7 mm` NPTH `H1–H4` secured to `MECH-LID-BOT` bosses with **nylon `M2.5 × 6 mm` screws** (`MECH-FAST-2`). Completely breaks any chassis-to-GND fault path if an LED wire pinches against the aluminum core. |
| **3A (Refined)** | USB-C cable entry & dew protection | Noted trap is used in garden (laptop charger) or portable (power bank), never deliberately in rain, only light dew; rejected proprietary waterproof cable and TPU printing in favor of a cheap off-the-shelf rubber grommet. | Added a flat-faced **`Ø20.2 × 2.5 mm` grommet flange** (`Ø23.6 mm` inner retention groove) on `MECH-CAP-BOT` accepting an off-the-shelf **`£0.30` `20 mm` rubber membrane grommet** (e.g., Eaton `KT-M20` / RS `06664634` / SES `20 mm DG`), radially slit to wrap around any standard USB-C cable + recommended acrylic **conformal coating** (e.g. MG Chemicals `419D`) on `driver_pcb`. |
| **4A** | LED MCPCB dielectric thermal conductivity | Selected **4A** (standard `1.0 W/m·K` Al MCPCB). | Kept JLCPCB standard **`1.0 W/m·K` `20 × 80 × 1.6 mm` Al MCPCB** (`ENIG`). Thermal FVM confirms `T_j,UV = 60.4 °C` at `30 °C` horizontal (`+39.6 °C` margin) and `65.3 °C` at `35 °C` (`+34.7 °C` margin). |
| **S1-A** | Mechanical simplification (Eliminate bottom heatsink & all CNC milling) | *"OK, I love the reduction in milled aluminium parts... S1A, S2A, S3."* | Eliminated `MECH-HS-EXT-BOT`, CNC-milled `MECH-ADAPT-BOT`, `MECH-TIM-PCB` gap pad, bottom `4× M3×14` hub bolts, and Kapton pocket lining. Replaced with 3D-printed flat **`MECH-LID-BOT`** featuring `4×` integral `11.5 mm` `M2.5` PCB bosses (`9.7 mm` air under TH pins). Switched `MECH-ADAPT-TOP` to stock **`Ø76.2 mm` (3") × `3 mm` Al disc** and `MECH-ANCHOR-TOP/BOT` to stock **`32 × 32 mm` Al square bar** -> **ZERO CNC milling across the entire lamp**, lamp height drops `62 mm` (`288 mm` vs `350 mm`), and amortized UK BOM drops to **`£98.49` ex-VAT**. |
| **S3** | Mode switch dry-circuit reliability & simplification | Approved **S3** (`C&K 7211SYZBE` gold DPDT On-On-On). | Replaced silver `SPDT` + `BAT54`/`BZT52C5V1`/`22 kΩ` with gold dry-circuit **`C&K 7211SYZBE`** (`0.4 VA max @ 20 V`, `1/4-40` bushing fitting `C&K 759D02000` boot) wired directly to `J4` + `R5/R6 = 1.0 kΩ` + `D15/D16` `5 V` ESD diodes. |

## 7. Rev 4.2 — Second Independent Review (LED Subsystem: Carbon + Opus 5.5) & 1-Off Hobby Cost-Down

Following Rev 4.1, a second independent parallel adversarial review (**Carbon** + **Opus 5.5**) audited the LED packaging, DIY soldering, and MCPCB thermal path alongside a user-driven focus on **true single-unit (1-off) hobby checkout cost** rather than amortized batch cost.

### 7.1 Reviewer Findings & Convergence

| Theme | Carbon & Opus 5.5 Independent Findings | Decision in Rev 4.2 |
|---|---|---|
| **1. `1.0 W/m·K` MCPCB Dielectric Bottleneck & DIY Reflow Risk** | Both independently proved `15.29 K/W` (`90.3%` of `T_j,UV - T_core`) was burned across the `100 µm` `1.0 W/m·K` dielectric (`L_spread = 1.2 mm`, making `>75%` of the copper islands thermally inactive). Realistic `50%` DIY contiguous thermal-pad voiding + `64 mm` span bow pushed hot-night `T_j,UV` to `101–103.6 °C` (`> 100 °C` abs max), while unbudgeted stencil/paste/hotplate tooling added `£25–£70+` and `Pad 3` (`NC`) voiding failed silently on diode checks. Both verified `SST-10-UV` and `Cree XP-E2` have **electrically neutral thermal pads** (`100%` DTP-safe on a shared Al core with `M3` nylon screw washers). | **Eliminated custom `led_mcpcb` and bare-SMD reflow completely.** Switched to **`8×` off-the-shelf pre-mounted `20 mm` Copper DTP LED stars** (`R_sub ≈ 0.50 K/W`, cutting `T_j,UV` by `-10` to `-20 °C`, removing the second custom PCB and all reflow gear). |
| **2. Assembly-Guide Soldering Sequence Bug (Carbon)** | `ASSEMBLY_GUIDE.md` Step 3 previously instructed bolting MCPCBs onto the aluminum core *before* soldering `22 AWG` wires, sinking `~31 W` out of the soldering iron into the core. | Fixed in Rev 4.2: **pre-wire all `8×` copper stars on a silicone/wood mat *before* applying thermal paste or bolting to `MECH-HS-CORE`**, using pre-crimped `22 AWG` JST-XH pigtails (eliminating a JST crimper too). |
| **3. 1-Off Mechanical Cost & Separate Top Heatsink (User + Thermal Solver)** | User questioned why 1-off mechanical cost was `£90.22` ex-VAT (`67%` was multi-pack fasteners/inserts/seals, consumables, and the top heatsink/anchor/eye-bolt stack) and asked whether the `40 × 40 × 3 mm` aluminum core itself could shed the heat. Coupled thermal modeling proved an **`85.5 mm` bare protruding `40 × 40 × 3 mm` tube** (`250 mm` exact stock UK cut — zero sawing, zero paint!) paired with Copper DTP stars passes **16/16 real-WPE modes (`22–30 °C`)** and **4/4 real-WPE modes (`35 °C`)** with `T_j,UV = 53 °C` (`22 °C` UK night) / `60 °C` (`30 °C` hot night). Open side-vented chimney was evaluated and explicitly rejected as unnecessary complexity vs a sealed press-fit top rain cap. | **Removed `SK 578/50 SA`, `MECH-ADAPT-TOP` Al disc, `MECH-ANCHOR-TOP/BOT`, `MECH-EYE-1`, spray paint, shims, `M4` hardware, and all brass heat-set inserts.** Kept **1 single metal part** (`250 mm` bare `40 × 40 × 3 mm` Al tube) + drilled `Ø8 mm` hanging hole + 3D-printed `MECH-PLUG-TOP` rain cap, `MECH-PLUG-BOT`, captive `M3` hex-nut pockets, printed `Ø2.45 mm` PCB locating pins, and snap-on `MECH-DRILL-JIG`. |
