All automated electrical, PCB/Gerber, SPICE, thermal, CAD (including ray-cast wall-breach & 14-insert unified-M3 B-Rep checks), and BOM verification gates pass with zero open blockers via `python3 verify_all.py --check`. ## Routine Pre-Order Checks

| Item | What to Check Before Ordering |
|---|---|
| **1. JLCPCB 2-Reel Hybrid HASL PCBA (`driver_pcb`)** | Upload [driver_pcb_gerbers.zip](../pcb_design/gerbers/driver_pcb/driver_pcb_gerbers.zip), [driver_pcb_bom_pcba_hybrid.csv](../pcb_design/driver_pcb_bom_pcba_hybrid.csv), and [driver_pcb_cpl_hybrid.csv](../pcb_design/driver_pcb_cpl_hybrid.csv) (**Lead-Free HASL** finish, Top-Side Economic PCBA, Qty `2`). JLCPCB assembles **`41` SMDs** (`20` Basic + **ONLY `2` Extended reels**: `U1/U2` `C125330` & `U3` `C42459160`); select **"Remove order number"** for a clean Emperor-moth back silkscreen and verify polarized part orientation in JLCPCB's 3D viewer. *(Optional industrial upgrade for sub-zero or `> 30 °C` builds: swap `U4` from Basic `LM393DR2G` `C7955` to `LM2903DR2G` `C57474`.)* |
| **2. Farnell UK 1-Basket DIY Order** | Consolidate all **`11` DIY PCB parts** in [driver_pcb_diy_hand_solder.csv](../pcb_design/driver_pcb_diy_hand_solder.csv) (`4` THT `J1..J4` + `7` chunky 2-pad SMDs: `F1` `046801.5NRHF`, `D11` `Littelfuse SMBJ22A`, `L1/L2` `Bourns SRN6045-680M`, `R3/R4` `Bourns CRL1206-FW-R300ELF`, `R7` `Vishay CRCW25121R00FKEG`) together with `SW1` (`C&K 7211SYZBE`) and `SW1_BOOT` (`759D02000`) in one Farnell UK checkout. |
| **3. Convoylight.com 1-Vendor LED Stars (`D1..D8`)** | Order all 8 LEDs pre-mounted on **`20 mm` Copper DTP stars** in one Convoylight.com order: `4× Luminus SST-10-UV 365 nm`, `2× Osram KB CSLNM1.14` (`455 nm` Royal Blue), `1× Osram KP CSLNM1.F1` (Pure Green), and `1× Osram KW CSLNM1.TG` (`6500 K` Cool White). Verify electrically neutral thermal pads with a multimeter on arrival. |
| **4. Extruded XT PMMA Tube & `Soudal Fix ALL Crystal`** | Order `Ø60 × 54 × 3.0 mm` **extruded clear XT PMMA** (non-UV-blocked; cut to `115 ± 2 mm` in workshop) — **never** UV-blocking cast `GS` or polycarbonate — and run the quick 365 nm white-paper fluorescence bench check in [ASSEMBLY_GUIDE.md](ASSEMBLY_GUIDE.md) before cutting. Never clean PMMA with IPA! Click & collect **`Soudal Fix ALL Crystal`** (`MECH-SEAL-1`, Screwfix `51412`) and apply **only** to plastic-to-aluminum joints (never onto PMMA). |
| **5. 3D Printing, Bare Al Core & Unified Workshop `M3` Stock** | Print all 8 support-free parts in **Black PETG or ASA** (avoid plain PLA; Signal-Orange PETG/ASA for `MECH-HANG-BUSHING`). Leave the `30 × 30 × 3.0 × 200 mm` Al core **unpainted bare mill finish** (`£0` paint!). Confirm workshop stock of `14×` standard `M3 × 5.0 × 4.2 mm` brass inserts, `32×` `M3` CSK screws (`22× M3×8`, `2× M3×10`, `8× M3×12`), `20×` `M3` steel flat washers, thermal paste, and a `25`-pack of `M3` DIN 125 nylon insulating washers (`20` used: `16` stars + `4` PCB). |
