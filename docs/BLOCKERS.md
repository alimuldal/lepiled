# LepiLED DIY Maxi Switch — Pre-Order & Build Checklist

All automated electrical, PCB/Gerber, SPICE, 8-node thermal, CAD, and BOM verification gates pass via `python3 verify_all.py --check`. For the revision history and design rationale, see [DESIGN_DECISIONS.md](DESIGN_DECISIONS.md); for the change-propagation procedure, see [CHANGE_CHECKLIST.md](CHANGE_CHECKLIST.md).

## Routine Pre-Order Checks

| Item | What to Check Before Ordering |
|---|---|
| **1. JLCPCB 2-Reel Hybrid HASL PCBA (`driver_pcb`)** | Upload [driver_pcb_gerbers.zip](../pcb_design/gerbers/driver_pcb/driver_pcb_gerbers.zip), [driver_pcb_bom_pcba_hybrid.csv](../pcb_design/driver_pcb_bom_pcba_hybrid.csv), and [driver_pcb_cpl_hybrid.csv](../pcb_design/driver_pcb_cpl_hybrid.csv) (Lead-Free HASL finish, Top-Side Economic PCBA, Qty `2`). JLCPCB populates `41` SMDs (`20` Basic + `2` Extended reels: `U1/U2` `C125330` and `U3` `C42459160`). Select "Remove order number" for a clean back silkscreen and verify part orientations in the 3D viewer. |
| **2. Farnell UK DIY Order** | Order the `11` DIY PCB parts in [driver_pcb_diy_hand_solder.csv](../pcb_design/driver_pcb_diy_hand_solder.csv) (`4` THT `J1..J4` + `7` 2-pad SMDs: `F1` `046801.5NRHF`, `D11` `Littelfuse SMBJ22A`, `L1/L2` `Bourns SRN6045-680M`, `R3/R4` `Bourns CRL1206-FW-R300ELF`, `R7` `Vishay CRCW25121R00FKEG`) together with `SW1` (`C&K 7211SYZBE`) and `SW1_BOOT` (`759D02000`). Note Farnell's small-order handling fee below `£40` ex-VAT if not combined with other items. |
| **3. Convoylight.com LED Stars (`D1..D8`) & Thermal Epoxy** | Order all 8 LEDs pre-mounted on `20 mm` Copper DTP stars (`4× Luminus SST-10-UV 365 nm`, `2× Osram KB CSLNM1.14`, `1× Osram KP CSLNM1.F1`, `1× Osram KW CSLNM1.TG`) and `5 g` of 2-part thermally conductive epoxy (`Arctic Alumina` or `MG Chemicals 8329TFS`). Verify electrically neutral thermal pads with a multimeter on arrival. |
| **4. Extruded XT PMMA Tube, O-Rings & `80 ml` Neutral Sealant** | Order `Ø60 × 54 × 3.0 mm` non-UV-blocked extruded clear XT PMMA (ideally pre-cut to `115 ± 2 mm`) from a UK technical plastics supplier and confirm `365 nm` transmission with the split-beam white-paper test in [ASSEMBLY_GUIDE.md](ASSEMBLY_GUIDE.md). Do not clean PMMA with IPA. Order `2×` `60 × 3.5 mm` VMQ50/60 shroud O-rings, `1×` `88 × 2.5 mm` VMQ50 axial top-bell O-ring, `8 × 4 mm` silicone tubing, `Ø8 mm` ePTFE vent patch, and an `80 ml` hand-squeeze tube of `Soudal Fix ALL Crystal`. |
| **5. 3D Printing, `300 × 2 mm` Anodized Al Core & Hardware** | Print all 6 support-free parts in White PETG or White ASA (avoid plain PLA; `mech_cable_clamp.stl` already includes both mirrored L+R halves and `mech_plug_top.stl` includes both `6.0 mm` bail spools). Order a `300.0 mm` length of `30 × 30 × 2.0 mm` 6063-T6 **clear/silver-anodized** aluminum square tube (or paint a bare mill tube matte white/black, leaving the 8 star pads abraded bare), a `2.0 m` standard supple TPE/silicone left/right `90°` USB-C PD cable (`60 W+`), `2 m` of `24 AWG` silicone wire, `2× DIN 912 M4 × 50 mm` A2 stainless SHCS + `2×` M4 washers (top bolt) + `2×` M4 nyloc nuts (bottom nut locks in captive `-X` hex pocket; hanging bail bent from salvaged `~2.0–2.5 mm` coat-hanger steel wire), and confirm workshop stock of `6×` `M3 × 5.0` brass inserts, `4×` DIN 934 `M3` hex nuts, `10×` `M3` screws (`4× M3×12` CSK, `4× M3×8` pan, `2× M3×12` pan), steel washers, `12×` `M3` DIN 125 nylon washers (from 25-pack), and `1.0 mm` silicone thermal gap pad + `15 × 15 × 0.5 mm` brass shims. |
