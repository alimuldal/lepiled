# LepiLED DIY Maxi Switch — Mechanical & Thermal Design

## 1. Enclosure & Open-Chimney Cooling Architecture

The lamp is a fanless lantern (`323.0 mm` body height, `~588 g` White ASA) built around an open `30 × 30 × 2.0 × 300.0 mm` bare mill-finish aluminum square tube cooling chimney (`ε_IR ≈ 0.10`), six support-free pre-oriented (`Z = 0`) White ASA 3D-printed parts, an extruded clear XT PMMA shroud, epoxy-bonded Copper DTP LED stars (zero aluminum tapping), a captive `15 × 15 mm` silicone-pad and epoxied brass-shim PCB thermal bridge, `6` `M3` brass inserts + `4` captive `M3` hex nuts + `10` `M3` screws, and two `DIN 912 M4 × 50 mm` A2 stainless socket-head cross-bolts. For design history and revision rationale, see [DESIGN_DECISIONS.md](../docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md).

- **Open-Chimney Tapered Bottom Nosecone (`MECH-CAP-BOT`, `Z = 0.0..34.0 mm`):** `44.4°` tapered frustum (`Ø40.0 → Ø71.0 mm` over `15.8 mm`, `2.91 mm` core-socket corner wall, `2.80 mm` wall behind `MECH-GASKET-1` `60 × 3.5 mm` VMQ50 O-ring) topped by a `45°` outward watershed lip bevel. Features an open, unobstructed `22 × 22 mm` (`R3`, `4.76 cm²`) chimney air inlet beneath the aluminum core seat (`Z = 5.0 mm`), locked by a single `DIN 912 M4 × 50 mm` A2 stainless SHCS at `Z = 11.5 mm` (`Z_rel = 6.5 mm`, `Ø7.0 mm` head seated in the `+X` pocket driving a DIN 985 `M4` nyloc nut captured inside an anti-rotation `7.36 mm` AF `-X` hex pocket, with a `2.15 mm` solid waterproof bulkhead below the shroud bore), and a backed `1.8 × 2.5 mm` rounded-square neutral-sealant V-groove (`2.24 mm` corner hub wall) on top of the `Ø49 mm` central hub (`Z = 31.5..34.0 mm`) sealing the core socket from the optical chamber before PMMA installation. Prints upright on `Z = 0` with `0.000 mm²` overhang.
- **Top Electronics Chassis (`MECH-TOP-BASE`, `Z = 120.0..224.8 mm`, exported flat on `Z = 0`):** Upper `Ø71.0 mm` shroud socket (`MECH-GASKET-2` + uncrossed `±Y` `9.0 × 6.5 mm` JST-XH wire channels with `>= 2.0 mm` hub corner walls and `0.4 mm` core-socket elephant-foot chamfer) flaring at `45°` to a `26.0 mm` tall `Ø94.0 mm` side-port ring (slimmed `Ø84.0 mm` dry bay between reinforced `+Y`/`-X` bosses, corbelled at `45°` back to `Ø78.0 mm` under the `G3` deck) housing:
  - **`+X` Braced Vertical PCB Cradle, Captive `M3` Hex Nuts & `15.6 × 15.6 mm` Thermal-Bridge Chute:** Solid `1.5 mm` insulating barrier (`X = 15.55..17.05 mm`) shielding `J1`/`J4` pins, twin `3.0 mm` `45°` triangular side buttresses (`|Y| = 15.6..18.6 mm`, `X = 5.5..15.55 mm`) stiffening the vertical cradle against open-air JST mating and high-Z CoreXY ringing, two `9.0 mm`-wide vertical stanchions (`Y = ±14.0 mm`) with `45°`-roofed side-entry `2.70 × 5.95 mm` pockets holding `4×` DIN 934 `M3` hex nuts (`1.00 mm` unbroken back wall behind `M3 × 8` screws with `3×` nylon + steel washers giving `+0.85 mm` tip clearance, `1.65 / 1.30 mm` front walls, `Ø3.70 mm` ASA-tolerant PCB bores), and a closed-perimeter `15.6 × 15.6 mm` captive chute (`Z = 199.0..214.4 mm` + `45°` gable roof topped by `2.60 mm` solid plastic) directly behind `U1/U2` (`B.Cu` at `X = 20.9 mm`, `5.9 mm` nom / `5.7 mm` WC gap to the aluminum core) holding two `1.0 mm` dielectric silicone pads sandwiching `15 × 15 × 0.5 mm` brass shims. `J1` sits at deck level (`Z_mouth = 171.32 mm`, `26.32 mm` above the `Z = 145.0 mm` bay floor) with its wide slot along `±Y` so a standard left/right `90°` USB-C cable (`<= 18.0 mm` plug) mates in open air (`3.08 mm` clear of baffle) and curves smoothly to `+Y`.
  - **`+Y` Compressing Split Cable Clamp (`MECH-CABLE-CLAMP`):** `18.0 mm`-tall (`Z = 148..166 mm`, `1.86 mm` gable cheek, `3.0 mm` wall beneath `G3`) `45°`-gable port passing an intact straight charger-end USB-C plug outward during assembly, with a full-height `Y = 37.0..47.0 mm` internal boss wall (zero overhang notch), a fused `9.0 mm` internal dry-bay zip-tie strain-relief post, and a pre-mirrored L+R `45°`-wedge split clamp (`37.0 mm` wide flange with `2.75 mm` outer slot rim, `1.2 mm` axial flange standoff so wedges drive real radial compression, `5.5 × 3.4 mm` X-slotted `M3 × 12` pan-head bolt slots into `7.0 mm`-deep volcano-chamfered blind `M3 × 5.0` inserts with `3.00 mm` solid dry-bay wall and `+1.50 mm` bottom margin even fully closed, and a `10.2 mm × Ø9.0 mm` chamber giving `77.5%` incompressible fill on a `10 mm` slit `Ø8 × Ø4 mm` silicone sleeve with a thin neutral sealant bead on the flange face). Sits `4.8 mm` inside the `Ø118 mm` eaves and `4.5 mm` below the eaves underside.
  - **`-X` Recessed Draining `SW1` Cove (`C&K 7211SYZBE` + `759D02000` Boot):** `5.5 mm` recessed `32.0 mm`-wide alcove backed by a widened `34.0 mm` internal boss (`>= 2.24 mm` wet-to-dry wall across the full cove width; `X = -41.5 mm`, `sw1_z = 154.2 mm`, `18°` outward-sloping draining floor from `Z = 146.0 mm`, continuous `45°` ceiling from `Z = 162.05 mm`, giving `1.20 mm` top and `1.55 mm` bottom margin around the `Ø13.3 mm` silicone boot flange while preserving `1.45 mm` vertical / `1.03 mm` normal solid wall under the `G3` groove corner) with a true round `Ø6.50 mm` circular bore, internal blind bushing keyway (leaving the outer boot face 100% unbroken `360°`), and internal anti-rotation pocket (`8.33 mm` rear lug clearance to worst-case Al core), keeping the boot `3.4–5.3 mm` inside `MECH-CAP-TOP`'s `-X` visor brow (`X = -63.5 mm`).
  - **`-Y` Downward-Draining Breather Vent (`MECH-VENT-1`):** `Ø9.5 × 0.8 mm` flat horizontal pocket on the dry-bay floor (`Y = -33.8 mm`) over a `35°` downward-outward `Ø3.5 mm` bore exiting the `45°` underside flare (`Y = -38.9, Z = 136.9 mm`) so water drains by gravity.
  - **Axial Face O-Ring Deck & `4×` Open-Slot Gusseted Bolt Ears (`R = 51.0 mm`):** Flat `Z = 171.0 mm` deck carrying a `3.4 × 2.00 mm` layer-quantized axial face groove (`MECH-GASKET-3`, `88 × 2.5 mm` VMQ50, `20.0%` squeeze, `72.2%` fill) backed by a uniform `2.0 mm` vertical outer rim and `2.0 mm` centering pilot lip, surrounded by `4×` extended gusseted flush `M3 × 12` CSK ears (`Z = 149.2..163.0 mm` with `2.0 mm` full-width vertical collar and `2.25 mm` solid rim) featuring open outward radial U-slot access tunnels (`6.6 mm` wide, `45°` gable roof, zero razor feathers or floating slivers, `32`-waypoint upward insertion verified).
- **Top Weatherproof Rain Bell (`MECH-CAP-TOP`, `Z = 171.0..257.0 mm`, exported flat on `Z = 0`):** Overhanging `Ø118.0 mm` circular rain eaves with a monolithic `1.4 mm` support-free `45°` underside drip kerf (`R = 55.0..56.4 mm`, `1.40 mm` Layer-1 web to the insert volcano chamfers, `2.60 mm` solid outer rim bridged at visor/clip), volcano-chamfered `6.5 mm` deep eaves insert bores, an elastic `L = 10.0 mm` (`t = 1.70 mm` unbroken arm, `3.63 mm` throat, `~0.9%` ASA strain) `+Y` snap-in cable C-clip + zip-tie slot, and `-X` rain visor brow with `0.50 mm`-deep debossed `OFF / UV / ALL` labels and `0 / 1 / 2` tactile ribs; straight `Ø85.0 mm` bore (`9.78 mm` wire-bend space behind `J2`/`J3`) with an outer-taper-contoured `0.90 mm` (`2`-wall) translucent internal dimple directly opposite `D12` (`Y = +16.0, Z = 190.9 mm`); unbroken parallel `45.0°` lofted rain roof (`-28.2 cm³` lighter, `0` roof/bell/collar holes); and `10.0 mm` vertical `40.4 × 40.4 mm` collar with `0.8 mm` watershed top chamfer and `R3.0` rounded-corner `2.5 × 3.0 mm` neutral-sealant V-groove (`>= 1.35 mm` flat top dam all `360°`).
- **Open-Chimney Bare Mill-Finish Al Core & Ventilated Rain Finial (`MECH-HS-CORE`, `MECH-PLUG-TOP`):** Standard `300.0 mm` length of `30 × 30 × 2.0 mm` bare mill-finish 6063-T6 tube (`181 g`, `26 × 26 mm` open bore, `48.0 mm` external protrusion above collar, `ε_IR ≈ 0.10`; optional matte exterior spray paint on the upper `160 mm` exterior `Z = 145..305 mm` — keeping the bore interior and `365 nm` UV chamber unpainted — raises `ε_IR ≈ 0.85` for `-3.8 to -4.9 °C` extra margin on hot `35 °C` nights). `MECH-PLUG-TOP` drops 4 `C1.2` (`1.2 × 45°`) outer-chamfered gusseted corner legs (`23.5 mm` deep, clearing extruded Al bore inner corner fillets, `4.0 mm` solid wall above the `M4` teardrop apex), seats its stepped rim onto the tube top, opens `4× 14.4 × 9.0 mm` exhaust ports (`5.10 cm²` net of hood-root gussets) under an overhanging `38 × 38 mm` `45°` rain hood, and exports pre-flipped onto `Z = 0` linked by `0.40 mm` breakaway bed tabs to `2×` symmetric `6.0 mm` flanged `M4` bail spools (`1.00 mm` `5`-layer flanges, `Ø7.2 mm` barrel, `Ø4.4 mm` bore) clamped outside the `±X` aluminum tube walls by the top `DIN 912 M4 × 50 mm` A2 stainless SHCS cross-bolt + `2× M4` washers + nyloc nut (`48.6 mm` total stack) to carry a bent `~2.0–2.5 mm` steel coat-hanger wire hanging bail (`~Ø8 mm` closed eyes riding cleanly on the spools over the stainless bolt).

> **Why White ASA Is Standard (PETG Retired for General Field Use):** Under realistic square-duct chimney losses, `30 °C` worst-case core contact reaches `73.6 °C` (exceeding PETG's `70 °C` unloaded HDT-B and `55–65 °C` loaded bolt/O-ring creep onset) and `MECH-CAP-BOT` / `MECH-TOP-BASE` inner faces receive watt-level `365 nm` UV-A exposure. **White ASA** (`HDT-B ~95–100 °C`, `HDT-A ~85–92 °C`, UV-stable) maintains `>= 15 °C` structural margin even in `35 °C` heatwaves. Print `mech_drill_jig` in ASA, PETG, or PLA.

All `8/8` vertical UK-night (`<= 22 °C`) and `12/12` vertical `15–30 °C` Real-WPE cases pass **White ASA (`<= 80 °C` derated)**, LED, PMMA, and **`U4 LM393DR2G` (`<= 70 °C`)** on bare mill-finish aluminum. At `35 °C` heatwave worst-case, White ASA structure (`78.3 °C`), LEDs (`79.7 °C`), and PMMA (`48.5 °C`) remain safe, while bare-tube `T_pcb_avg` (`70.6 °C`) edges above `LM393`'s `70 °C` commercial rating; applying the optional **upper-tube exterior matte spray paint (`Z = 145..305 mm`, `ε = 0.85`)** lowers `35 °C` WC to `T_plastic = 73.6 °C`, `T_pcb_avg = 66.3 °C`, and `T_j,U4 = 67.0 °C` (**16/16 `15–35 °C` PASS**).

**Operating Guidance (Chimney Draft & Orientation):** The open chimney provides `~13–17 °C` of passive cooling at `~0.11–0.13 Pa` buoyancy head. **Always hang the lamp vertically** and keep both the bottom `22 × 22 mm` nosecone inlet and top finial exhaust ports clear of surfaces, funnel hubs, and debris. Sustained horizontal `8-ALL` operation stalls chimney draft (`Q_bore = 0`), reverses bridge heat flow into the PCB (`+0.07 to +0.12 W`), and pushes `30 °C` worst-case core/PCB temperatures to `89.8 / 77.4 °C` (exceeding `LM393` and approaching ASA/sealant limits; use `4-UV` only if temporarily laid horizontally).
